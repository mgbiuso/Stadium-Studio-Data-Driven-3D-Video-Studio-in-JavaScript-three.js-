A title with fact lines under it and a leader line to the exact spot it names, drawn over the 3D picture.

**Use it** for the one or two facts a shot is about: the building's name and opening date, the number the video turns on. Never more than one label per slot (top, mid, low) and never more than two in a frame.

**You provide**
- `title`: capitals, 2 to 4 words (`PENNSYLVANIA STATION`). Set in `layer-title`, or `layer-title-small` when a second label shares the frame.
- `sub`: 1 or 2 fact lines in `fact-line`, capitals, each with its source in the description (`563,795 SUBWAY ENTRIES`, `NOV 1–14 · MTA DATA`).
- `p`: the spot in the scene the leader points to.
- `slot`: `top`, `mid` or `low` (16%, 42% or 64% of the frame height, 6% in from the left).

**Look**: `white` text and lines over a `label-stroke` outline; the leader ends in a 4px dot on the spot. In then-vs-now shots, past-era labels may use `granite` for the title.

**Don't** put a label inside the apps' zones, cover the building with it, or add a box behind it.
