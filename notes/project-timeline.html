  1. STOP 1

    Model foundations

    NowTarget: TBD

    Yankee Stadium and Madison Square Garden look good enough to put on camera, and they are solid templates so the next venue takes a fraction of the time.

    Done so far

    • Reusable engine: one shared core, one recipe per venue
    • Data recipe for any NYC venue (city data, OpenStreetMap, transit)
    • MSG rebuilt from data and photos, inside and out
    • Yankee Stadium placed in its real neighborhood
    • Realistic lighting, corner shading, night glow
    • Stage 2 city: facades by age, water tanks, trees, curbs
    • Four iso icon looks, blueprint poster, cutaway
    • Real train timetables and the live MTA helper

    Next steps

    • You review every view on both venues and list what still looks off
    • Yankee details from the photos: frieze, flags, Monument Park, Great Hall
    • Fill in the Yankee Stadium notes file
    • Check the fly-through and camera glides on your Mac
    • Street life: cars, cabs and people (the last Stage 2 item)
    • Lock both venues in as the template

    Done whenyou would happily use any view of either venue in a video, and a new venue can be added in under a day.

  2. STOP 2

    Visual identity

    NextTarget: TBD

    Decide what makes a video recognizably yours at a glance.

    Next steps

    • Pick 2 or 3 signature looks to use in every video
    • Choose the title and caption type style
    • Set a small color palette for on-screen text
    • Make a short intro and ending card with your handle

    Done whena frame from any video could only be yours.

  3. STOP 3

    Content direction

    NextTarget: TBD

    Choose the lane and the repeatable formats, then write the first batch.

    Next steps

    • Settle the lane (starting idea: the hidden stories of places, stadiums first)
    • Pick 2 or 3 series formats, such as "what is underneath" and "then vs now"
    • Write the first 5 scripts (the MSG and Penn Station draft is ready)
    • Build the 1910 Penn Station model for the then-vs-now video
    • Decide how the NHL shot data comes back as a story

    Done whenfive scripts are written and you know what the next twenty are about.

  4. STOP 4

    Production pipeline

    LaterTarget: TBD

    Make one video take hours, not days.

    Next steps

    • Record button in Stadium Studio that saves clean vertical clips
    • Saved camera paths for each signature shot
    • Voice workflow: your mic, then Adobe Enhance Speech
    • A CapCut project template with captions and music set up
    • Test Blender for one or two hero shots

    Done whena full video goes from script to export in one sitting.

  5. STOP 5

    Launch

    LaterTarget: TBD

    Start posting on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, and learn from every video.

    Next steps

    • Set up the three accounts with one handle (steps in the video guide)
    • Post the first batch of 3 to 5 videos
    • Reply to comments in the first hour
    • Read each video's retention chart after 48 hours and fix one thing

    Done whenyou are posting on a steady rhythm, 2 or 3 a week.
