Visual Design Project

From 3D model to posted video

Your first video, start to finish: the arena that sits on a train station. One small step at a time. Do the step, tap the green button, move on.

Set up→Story→Script→Model→Record→Voice→Edit→Post→Learn

36 steps. About 214 minutes start to finish. You can stop any time. Your ticks are saved.

Keys to remember

Cmd + Shift + 5Open the Mac screen recorder
Cmd + Ctrl + EscStop a screen recording
Cmd + Ctrl + FMake Chrome full screen
SpacePlay or pause in CapCut
Cmd + BSplit a clip in CapCut
Cmd + ZUndo

    Get the free tools

    Where: Your Mac browser.

    • Install CapCut for the desktop. This is your video editor.

    Where: script.md, under Hook.

    • Write one of your own.

  9. 9
    About 10 min

    Rewrite the script in your own words

    Where: script.md, under Draft script.

    • Follow the shape: hook, setup, twist, payoff, question.
    • Change any line that does not sound like you.
    • Read it out loud with a timer. Aim for 45 to 60 seconds.
    You will see: A script that sounds like you talking to a friend.
    Tip: About 140 spoken words fill 60 seconds. Shorter usually wins.
  10. 10

    Fact check every line

    Where: script.md next to your sources.

    • Match each date and number to a source.
    • Say opinions as questions, like the last line does.
    • Cut anything you cannot back up.
    You will see: Every fact in the script has a source.
    Tip: History comments are brutal about mistakes. One wrong date can take over the comments.
  11. 11
    About 5 min

    Check the shot list

    Where: script.md, under Shot list.

    • Read each row: which line, which style, which camera move, which on-screen text.

    • Aim for a new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
    You will see: A shot for every line of the script.
    Tip: Pick 2 or 3 styles you use in every video. That repeated look is your brand.

    Where: Library of Congress photos and Wikimedia Commons.

    • Copy the credit line into Photo credits in script.md.

    Part 5 of 9 · Set up the screen to record

    Where: Finder, stadium-studio, then double-click index.html.

  16. 16
    About 2 min

    Record one shot

    Where: Anywhere on your Mac.

    • Press Cmd + Shift + 5.
    • Click Record Selected Portion in the toolbar.
    • Drag a tall box over the arena, shaped like a phone. Keep the side panel out.
    • Click Record, then click a style and wait for the camera to stop.
    • Press Cmd + Ctrl + Esc to stop.
    You will see: A new video file on your Desktop.
    Tip: In the toolbar, Options, then Save to, lets you pick the clips folder instead.
  17. 17
    About 15 min

    Record every shot on the list

    Where: Same setup as the last step.

    • Record each row of the shot list as its own file.
    • Record each one twice. Keep the smoother one.
    • Rename them to match the list, like 03-cutaway.mov.
    • For a slow orbit, click Explore and drag slowly while recording.
    You will see: One clip per shot in the clips folder.
    Tip: Ask Claude for a record button that saves clean vertical clips straight from the page. It skips the cropping.
  18. 18
    About 3 min

    Blender, the other 3D option

    Where: Read this step. Nothing to click yet.

    • Our code engine: fast, same look every time, and it can power the map site later.
    • Blender: film-quality light, smooth camera paths, but slow to learn.
    • Best of both: Claude can add an export button so our models open in Blender.
    • City around the arena: the Blosm add-on brings in real buildings from OpenStreetMap.
    You will see: You know when to use which.
    Tip: Start videos with the code engine. Learn Blender on the side for the shots that need more polish.

    Where: Blender Guru on YouTube, the beginner donut series.

    • Stop after the parts on materials, camera and rendering.
    • You do not need the whole series.
    You will see: You can move around a scene, light it and render one picture.
    Tip: Credit OpenStreetMap in your caption whenever you use its map data.
  20. 20
    About 2 min

    Set Blender to vertical video

    Where: Blender, the Properties panel on the right, the printer icon (Output).

    • Set Resolution X to 1080.
    • Set Resolution Y to 1920.
    • Set Frame Rate to 30.

    Tip: Save this as your start file: File, Defaults, Save Startup File.

    About 10 min

    Part 6 of 9 · Record your voice

    Where: A quiet room. A closet full of clothes works great.

    • Plug in your mic. Keep it about a hand's width from your mouth.
    • Open QuickTime Player, then File, New Audio Recording.
    • Read the script three times in a row. Smile while you read.
    • Save to the voice folder.
    You will see: One audio file with three full takes.
    Tip: Later, for an AI voice, ElevenLabs is the common pick (paid plans start around $5 a month). Label it when you post.

    Clean up the audio

    Where: Adobe Podcast Enhance in your browser.

    • Upload your voice file.
    • Lower the strength slider to about 70 percent if it sounds robotic.
    • Download the cleaned file to the voice folder.
    You will see: Your voice sounds clear with no room echo.
    Tip: Claude can also clean it for you with the Adobe Enhance Speech connection.

    Where: CapCut on your Mac.

    • Click Create project.

    • Set the ratio above the preview to 9:16.

    About 15 min

    Lay the shots over the voice

    Where: CapCut timeline.

    • Put each shot above the line it matches.
    • Split clips with Cmd + B and delete the extra.
    • Change the shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
    • Give old photos a slow zoom so they feel alive.
    You will see: Pictures that change in time with your words.
    Tip: Your strongest shot goes first, with the hook written on it.
  25. 25

    Add captions

    Where: CapCut, the Captions or Text tab, then Auto captions.

    • Fix every wrong word, especially names.
    • Move them to the middle of the screen.
    You will see: Easy-to-read words that match your voice.
    Tip: Most people watch with the sound off. Captions are not optional.
  26. 26
    About 5 min

    Add music and sound

    Where: CapCut, plus your music source.

    • Download one track from the YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay Music.

    You will see: Music you can feel but your voice stays clear.

  27. 27
    About 3 min

    Check it on your phone

    Where: Export a test copy, AirDrop it to your phone, play it full screen.

    Tip: Keep words out of the bottom fifth and the right edge. The apps put buttons there.
