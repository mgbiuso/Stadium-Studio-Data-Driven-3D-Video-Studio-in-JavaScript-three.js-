## Draft script (rewrite it in your own words, about 120 words)
Hook: There's a train station hidden under this arena.
Setup: This is Madison Square Garden, home of the Rangers and the Knicks.
Twist: But look underneath. That's Penn Station, one of the busiest train stations in the country.
And it didn't always look like this. In 1910, Penn Station was a pink granite palace,
with a waiting room modeled on the ancient Roman baths.
In 1963, it was torn down. The Garden was built right on top.
Payoff: People were so upset that New York created its Landmarks Preservation Commission two years later.
That law later helped save Grand Central.
Question: So should the Garden move, and let Penn Station be rebuilt? Tell me below.
