## Points to be careful with

- **"4 Penn Plaza"**: the office tower built with the Garden is **Two Penn Plaza**, not 4. Don't repeat the thread's name.
- **"The back part of Grand Central was partially demolished for the MetLife Building"**: the MetLife (Pan Am) Building (1963) went up just north of the terminal, on the site of an older office building. Check the exact wording before using it.
- **"A subterranean maze performs the job just as effectively"**: opinion, and the opposite of how most viewers feel about today's Penn Station. Good tension for the comments, not a fact for the narration.

**The strongest beat for a 60-second video:** the trade. The railroad gave away its palace and got a free basement station back. That's a twist viewers will repeat in the comments.

## Script idea this suggests

The thread's best line of argument is money: *the air above the station was worth more than the station.* That could replace "To make room, New York tore down this" with something like:
"The air above Penn Station was worth more than the station itself. So in 1963, the railroad sold it, and tore down the palace underneath."
Or, built on the trade:
"In 1963, the railroad sold the air above its station. In return, it got a new one for free: underground."
